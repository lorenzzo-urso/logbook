Rethink what it means to grow older. In the second volume of his Intelligent Age Series, Klaus Schwab challenges outdated assumptions about aging, work, and purpose. With longer lifespans, today’s older adults are healthier, more educated, and more active than ever. Their experience and wisdom are invaluable, offering society the chance to harness and celebrate them. Longevity and Retirement in the Intelligent Age shows how businesses, communities, and individuals can harness the untapped potential of aging employees and retirees. When fully engaged, older adults can drive innovation, mentorship, and meaningful contributions that benefit everyone. This book also explores how technology and AI can power lifelong learning, skill-building, and productivity, turning retirement from an ending into the start of a purposeful, opportunity-filled chapter. Get ready to: Challenge conventional ideas about aging and discover how longer, healthier lives open doors to new opportunities. Tap into the skills, wisdom, and energy of older adults to drive impact in business, community, and personal life. Leverage AI and modern tools to keep learning, growing, and making a difference at any stage of life. Whether you’re approaching retirement, managing a multigenerational workforce, or simply curious about the future of aging, this book offers a roadmap to a vibrant, impactful second half of life. The future of aging isn’t about slowing down. It’s about stepping up.
